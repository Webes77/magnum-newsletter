## Step 1B: what the big newsletters all do (The Rundown, Superhuman, Evolving AI, Uncover AI)
1. Greeting, then straight into the lead story's sharpest fact in one line. "Welcome back, Superhuman. Jensen Huang's retirement may be one of the shortest in tech history." / "Welcome back, AI enthusiasts. Anthropic just pulled off the biggest IPO in history, briefly hitting a $2 trillion valuation."
2. Headlines say what happened in plain words, often subject plus verb plus result. "California takes OpenAI to court over agent escapes"; "Google's Gemini can now use your Mac for you."
3. Story openings carry who, what and a number in the first sentence. "OpenAI launched ChatGPT Wallet, letting its personal AI agents, called dots, make online purchases using your saved credit card."
4. Short, warm sign-off with names, no pitch. "See you soon, Rowan, Zach, Shubham, Jennifer, and Nate"; "Until next time, Zain, Theodore, & the Superhuman AI team."
Applied: opener leads on the most striking true detail ($389 bill, heat pump in peak pricing); headlines subject-verb-result; leads open on who/what/number. Difference from our rules: they all lead on the biggest industry news (Anthropic IPO, Nvidia). Our rule picks for the reader, so the IPO is not run. Noted for James, no change made.
