# CLAUDE.md

This repository is This Week in AI, Magnum AI's weekly client newsletter,
published from `main` at `https://webes77.github.io/magnum-newsletter/`.

If James asks you to publish this week's edition, or attaches a picture or
a video for The Magnum, follow **Publish from a chat** in `ROUTINE.md`
step by step. That is the normal way an edition goes live since
11 October 2026. Never tell him to save anything to Google Drive, write a
`notes.txt` or reply to an email; he hands the Magnum over in the chat.

Everything else about the routines, the schema and the manual publish
command is in `ROUTINE.md` and `tools/EDITION-SCHEMA.md`. No em dashes, never
the word "solid", Australian English.
